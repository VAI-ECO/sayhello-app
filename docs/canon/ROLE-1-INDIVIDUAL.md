# ROLE 1 — THE INDIVIDUAL

**The person who says hello. The core app.**

⚠️ Every other role is an addition to this one. §26.4 — roles are additive on a single account. There is never a second login.

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

Somebody who wants to meet the people already around them. Not a networker, not a dater, not a user of a social network — a person in a bar, a queue, a park, who would say hello if saying hello were easier.

The entire product exists to make one moment happen: two strangers, both nudged, both accepting, meeting in person.

**Everyone is 18+.** No exceptions today.

---

## 2. HOW THEY GET IN

**Phone plus a six-digit code. No passwords anywhere** (§26.1).

The account is created **at OTP verification** (§26.2), not at the end of onboarding — so nobody loses work by abandoning halfway. §26.7: answers save as they happen.

At signup they receive a **permanent Hello Prize Drawing Number** (§26.3). Its own column, separate from the internal user ID, and it never changes.

**Onboarding order:** phone → OTP → name and birthday → **colour → photo** → location → notifications → welcome.

⚠️ **Colour comes before photo.** Waverly is already wearing their colour when the photo screen opens.

**Under 18 is a hard stop.** One correction is allowed for a mistyped birth year; a second under-18 entry is a real dead end with no path back into signup.

⚠️ **Denied permission is not blocked** (§26.5). Limited mode plus one dismissible banner. Never a wall.

**The welcome is re-openable forever** (§26.6), and each point repeats contextually the first time that surface is reached.

---

## 3. WHAT THEY CAN DO

**Say hello.** The core loop, and everything else is secondary to it.

**Answer icebreakers.** A library of questions they write their own answers to. ⚠️ **Not tap-to-select.** Three answered unlocks sharing at reveal — a floor, not a cap. The gate is on **sharing**, not signup; a user completes onboarding with zero answers.

**Pick an identity colour.** One of ten. It becomes their pin on the map, their avatar ring, and their colour on merchandise. ⚠️ The picker is one screen. Waverly is shown already wearing each colour.

**Attend and host Hootzas.** Gatherings — a house party, a run club, a tailgate. Anyone can host.

**Turn on Romantic nudges.** ⚠️ Optional and off by default. Gender and "looking for" are matching input only and never displayed.

**Spend points in Waverly's Place.** 25 points is 20% off, 50 is 30%, 100 is 40%. ⚠️ Forty is a hard ceiling.

**Block and report.**

**Add another role.** Ambassador, Hangout owner, Organization — all additive on the same account (§26.4).

---

## 4. WHAT THEY CANNOT DO

⚠️ **This section matters more than the one above.** Most of the product is what it refuses to do.

| |
|---|
| **See who declined them.** §10.21 — the app never explains non-acceptance. A decline, a timeout, a block and a missed connection are indistinguishable from nothing happening. |
| **See a profile before mutual accept.** §10.6. A pending hello shows only Waverly. No name, no photo, no user ID, no location. |
| **See anything before the reveal.** Three hours after mutual accept, and not a moment earlier. |
| **Know how far away anybody is.** ⚠️ §8.12 — the UI never states a distance. Not in copy, a notification, an empty state, or marketing. |
| **Delete a pin.** §10.10 — the pin is permanent and survives account deletion. Both people put it there; one cannot remove the other's record of the meeting. |
| **Delete a selfie.** §10.11 — removal is a formal request handled out of band, never a button, never inside the encounter. |
| **Earn from the same person twice.** §4.2 — 25 points per unique person, once ever. |
| **Message somebody they have not met.** No DMs, no inbox, no friend requests. ⚠️ Not even from a Hootza chat — they are in the same building, so the hello happens in the room. |
| **Farm points.** No points in Ambassador mode (§4.12). The 10-activity count is recomputed from the ledger, never trusted from a counter (§4.18). |

## 5. MONEY

**They never pay anything.** The app is free to individuals.

**They earn points**, which are the only currency:

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Accept | 25 |
| Selfie | 25 |
| Continue | 50 |
| **Total per person** | **100** |
| Host a Hootza that completes | **10** (§4.19 · §28.5) — not if cancelled |
| Refer a Hangout that converts | **100** (§4.11) |
| Refer the app | **0** (§4.20) — retention only |

⚠️ **25 per unique person, once ever** (§4.2). **Solo max 75** — the selfie is joint-only (§4.3).

**One pool, two futures** (§4.4). Points are simultaneously drawing entries and store discount currency. ⚠️ **§4.5 — spending points destroys entries.**

**No reset until the first drawing** (§4.9). After it, reset to **1, never zero**, on **30-day cycles** anchored to that drawing. Everyone on the same clock. ⚠️ Nobody ever has nothing.

**Retention: ten activities per cycle** (§4.10). Before the first drawing: no retention requirement. A converted Hangout referral earns **100 points** and satisfies retention for that month and the following eleven (§4.11).

⚠️ **Balance is derived from the ledger, never stored** (§4.17).

---

## 6. WHAT THEY SEE

**What's Happening** is home. There is no separate Home tab. Tonight's Hootzas, discounts, Hollas, nearby Hello Hangouts. ⚠️ Only a Hello supersedes it — not a Discount, not a Holla, not a Hootza starting soon.

**The Hello Map.** Every pin they have made, and everybody else's. ⚠️ Pins carry **no names** in the map view. Name and selfie appear only on the enlarged view.

**Shape carries meaning, not colour**, because a person's identity colour IS their pin:

| Object | Shape |
|---|---|
| A person | Teardrop — and the only teardrop. **Solid.** |
| A Hello Hangout | Flat-bottomed square. Outlined. |
| A Hootza | Circle with a ring — the ring is a clock. Outlined. |
| A Holla | Pennant on a stem. Outlined. |
| A Discount | Tag, eyelet in the neck. Outlined. |

**People are solid. Places, events and things are outlined.**

**Tabs: Happening · Map · Hellos · Prizes · You.**

---

## 7. HOW THEY LEAVE

**Sign out** keeps everything.

**Delete the account**, and this is the part that must be stated plainly *before* they confirm:

| Goes, permanently | Stays |
|---|---|
| Name and photo | ⚠️ **Their pins on the Hello Map** |
| Icebreaker answers | ⚠️ **Selfies they are in** |
| Points, and any entries they would have been | |
| Chats | |

⚠️ **§10.10 — the pin survives account deletion, consented at signup.** Where their name was, a pin reads **"a Say Hello member."**

Selfies they appear in can be taken down by request, handled by a person, out of band (§10.11).

⚠️ **If a user is surprised after deleting, the consent at signup was not real.** The disclosure is the whole point.

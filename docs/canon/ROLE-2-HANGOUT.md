# ROLE 2 — THE HELLO HANGOUT

**The local business that pays to be on the map.**

⚠️ **"Hello Hangout" is never shortened to "Hangout."** Not in copy, not in a variable name, not baked into an asset. §7.17.

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

A coffee shop, a bar, a barber, a bowling alley. The places people already go.

**Say Hello only works where people are.** A proximity app with nowhere to be is nothing, so Hello Hangouts are not an advertising product bolted on — they are the density that makes the encounter possible.

⚠️ A Hello Hangout is a **place**. A Hootza is an **event with an end time**. They are different objects and they never merge.

**One Hello Hangout per location** (§1.13). Franchise volume discount is noted, not a rule.

---

## 2. HOW THEY GET IN

Two doors.

**An Ambassador walks in.** ⚠️ §1.11 — the Ambassador presents **two** options at the door and only two: **$199 now, or defer.**

**They sign up themselves** on the website, on an Ambassador's or Influencer's code, or with no code at all. §2.9 — every Hangout has an initiator; a blank code means Say Hello.

⚠️ **Nothing is due until after the first drawing** (§1.7). Deferred billing charges seven days after it (§1.18).

**No approval queue.** **No tablet at any tier** (§1.14).

---

## 3. WHAT THEY CAN DO

**Be found.** A listing, a page, a pin on the map — a flat-bottomed square, outlined, Fern `#4F8C36`. ⚠️ Founding Hangouts carry an **Amber `#CE8527`** badge, visible on the map pin, not only on the page.

**Post to What's Happening.** ⚠️ **Two free posts per calendar month. Founding gets three. No rollover. Additional posts $10** (§17.10). **A Hootza posted to the feed consumes a slot; a Hootza not posted is free and unlimited** (§17.20–17.21).

**Post a Discount.** ⚠️ **Two clocks** (§17.6): the post's 7-day shelf life AND the deal's own start and end dates, entered manually. Whichever expires first takes it down.

⚠️ §17.11 — **expiry does not return a slot.** Delete-and-repost burns one; edit does not.

**Post a Holla** — an announcement. Plum `#6A4A9C`. May link to a Special on their own page (§17.12).

**Host Hootzas.** Completing one awards the host **10 points** plus 1 per attendee who scans in — not if cancelled (§28.5).

**See their own page** with no expiry (§17.8) — posts leave the feed and stay on their page.

**Display a QR stand.** Customer scans are what prove the place is real and being used.

---

## 4. WHAT THEY CANNOT DO

| |
|---|
| ⚠️ **See who came.** A Hangout is not given a list of people. Selfies appear on their wall; the people do not become a mailing list. |
| ⚠️ **Remove a selfie taken at their venue.** §10.11 — "business cannot remove one." Not their pin, not their record. |
| **Post beyond their slots without paying.** §17.10. |
| **Post without an expiry.** §17.1 — enforced at creation. Nothing expired ever renders. |
| **Buy a discount for their customers.** §2.10 — codes carry credit, never price. No audience, creator or promo discount. |
| **Get a tablet.** §1.14. |
| **Reach a Founding price after the window closes.** §1.17. |

---

## 5. MONEY

| | |
|---|---|
| **Regular** | **$349/year** (§1.1) |
| **Founding** | **$199/year** — accepted at initial contact (§1.2) |
| **Deferred then converted** | **$249/year** — a $100 discount off $349 for five years, earned by the second Ambassador visit (§1.3) |

⚠️ **§1.4 — the $249 business IS a Founding Hangout.** Identical status, perks and lock. It is not a third price point.

**Founding price lock: five years** (§1.5). Badge: the golden Hello Hangout icon (§1.6).

**Framing: "under $30 a month"** (§1.9).

⚠️ **§1.12 — $249 is both a price and a margin. Every document must say which.**

### The Founding window

⚠️ **§1.15 — the Founding programme ends when the first drawing is ANNOUNCED**, not when it happens. Modelled as **one system-wide timestamp**, never a per-business flag (§1.16).

After that timestamp $199 and $249 both cease to be reachable. Existing Founding Hangouts keep everything (§1.17).

❓ **The first drawing date is not set** (§1.19). Three things depend on it.

### Dead figures — remove on sight

⚠️ **$399 · $299 · $300 · $60/$399 electronic package · "Founding Council free forever" · "First Movers 50% off" · "Early Access 20% off"** (§1.10).

### Hardship

A quiet petition, manual admin approval. ⚠️ **Never advertised** (§1.8).

### What their payment triggers

⚠️ **§2.14 — nobody in the chain is paid until the Hangout pays Say Hello.** Once a year. No renewal, nobody paid — Ambassador, Influencer, retired originator, all of them.

⚠️ **§14.8 — payment triggers the maintenance schedule.** Nobody services an unpaid Hangout.

---

## 6. WHAT THEY SEE

A dashboard: their page, their posts, their remaining slots, their renewal date, their Ambassador.

Selfies taken at their venue, on their wall (§10.19).

⚠️ **Not a customer list.** Not names, not contact details, not a way to reach anybody.

---

## 7. HOW THEY LEAVE

They stop renewing.

⚠️ **§2.14 — no renewal, nobody paid.** The Ambassador, the Influencer and any retired originator all stop earning on that Hangout.

The Hangout is **released** and enters the reassignment queue if the Ambassador rather than the business is the one lapsing — see `ROLE-3-AMBASSADOR.md` §16.

❓ **Unruled:** what happens to a lapsed Hangout's page, its posts, and the selfies on its wall.

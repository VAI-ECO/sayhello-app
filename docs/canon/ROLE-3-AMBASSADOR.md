# ROLE 3 — THE AMBASSADOR

**The field rep who walks into businesses and signs them up.**

⚠️ The most scattered role in the old canon — spread across §2, §14, §15, §16, FLOW-03, FLOW-11 and a cost comparison. This file collects all of it.

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

The reason there is anywhere to go. **Say Hello only works where people are**, and Ambassadors are the people who put the places on the map.

They walk into a coffee shop with an offer that costs the owner nothing to accept, sign them up, install a stand, and come back twice a year.

⚠️ **It is a role added to a personal account** (§26.4), never a separate login. An Ambassador is also an individual who says hello.

---

## 2. HOW THEY GET IN

Applications are reviewed. **Territories are limited.**

⚠️ **A W-9 is collected from every Ambassador at onboarding** (§14.15).

⚠️ **§16.15 — a new Ambassador with no signed Hangouts has no radius and receives no offers.** They start by signing somebody themselves.

**Payout provider is Trolley** (§14.12) — owns W-9/W-8, TIN validation, payout rails and 1099-NEC/1042-S. ⚠️ Do not build custom payout or tax infrastructure.

---

## 3. WHAT THEY CAN DO

**Sign up businesses.** ⚠️ §1.11 — two options at the door, only two: **$199 now, or defer.**

**Install QR stands.** §14.10 — installing a stand **is** a verified visit.

**Complete visits.** §14.5 — a visit requires **both GPS and a scan of that Hangout's QR.** Live camera only.

⚠️ §14.6 — **GPS is monitored, not tight. The scan is the real proof.** Recommend 200–300 m.

**Accept queue offers.** Released Hangouts are offered one at a time.

**Go into Away mode.** §14.25 — it means "skip me in the offer queue." That is all it does. ⚠️ **No advance notice, no approval, no cap, no yearly limit** (§14.26).

**Take on sub-Ambassadors** once their book exceeds 50 Hello Hangouts (§14.31). $12/visit (§14.32), controlled by Super Admin (§14.33).

**Sell their book**, with company sign-off and a permanent rule-of-two obligation (§14.24).

**Opt out**, and still be paid — see §5.

---

## 4. WHAT THEY CANNOT DO

| |
|---|
| ⚠️ **Earn points in Ambassador mode** (§4.12). No points from a hello with a business owner they originated, in either mode. |
| ⚠️ **Browse the queue.** §16.12 — offered one at a time, never a browsable list. It is not an entitlement. |
| ⚠️ **Reclaim an old area once reassigned** (§14.23). |
| **Skip a visit.** §14.1 — two per term year, every Hangout, regardless of book size. **No scaling, no reduction.** |
| **Be paid per visit.** §14.9 — service is paid at renewal. |
| **Withdraw immediately.** §14.13 — five days after the business's payment clears. §14.14 — **$200 minimum**, manual approval queue. |
| ⚠️ **Take referrals while behind.** §16.17 — ANY Hangout in the book six months without a visit makes them ineligible, **even if that Hangout is still inside its term year** (§16.18). Blocks influencer referrals, Say Hello leads and released-Hangout offers (§16.19). |

⚠️ §16.20 — **they are told, plainly, with what to do about it.** §16.21 — eligibility restores automatically when they catch up.

---

## 5. MONEY

### The protected number

⚠️ **§2.2 — $100/year per Hangout. Never reduced, for any reason.**

Split as **$20 origination** (the business pays) plus **$80 service** (the visit requirement met) — §2.1.

| Case | Year 1 | Every renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Signs cold | **$100** (§2.3) | **$100** (§2.5) |
| Somebody else originated, they service | **$80** (§2.4) | **$100** (§2.5) |

⚠️ **§2.14 — nobody is paid until the Hangout pays Say Hello.** Once a year. No renewal, nobody paid.

### Bonuses

**Creator-code bonus: $100, once ever, to the AMBASSADOR** — for signing up on an Influencer's code AND completing 10 Hangouts. Paid by Say Hello (§2.8).

### Opt-out

⚠️ **§14.16 — 20% of what the current Ambassador earns on that Hangout, for two years, on Hangouts they ORIGINATED only.** If the $100 moves, the opt-out follows it.

⚠️ **§14.17 — origination never transfers. Servicing does.** Two separate permanent fields.

§14.18 — opt-out is a **declared action**, never inferred. **Abandonment pays nothing.**

§14.19 — the screen shows real numbers, requires type-to-confirm, emails a copy.
§14.20 — **48-hour hold.** The position is held; nothing reassigned during the window.
⚠️ §14.21 — **the tone is warm. A door left open, not a warning.**
§14.22 — **no cut-off on return.** An Ambassador who left is always welcome back.

### Payouts

Trolley (§14.12). Five days after the business's payment clears (§14.13). **$200 minimum**, manual approval queue (§14.14).

---

## 6. WHAT THEY SEE

**The Ambassador panel** — territory, queue, earnings, code, toolkit.

**Origination date is displayed** in both Super Admin and the panel (§14.11).

⚠️ **EARNED must never look as certain as BUILDING.** That distinction is legal, not decorative.

**On a queue offer** they are told (§16.10): what kind of account it is, why it is available, what they earn, the reinstatement risk, the last visit, the last customer scan, and the renewal date.

⚠️ **§16.9 — THE REINSTATEMENT RULE IS INTERNAL ONLY.** Never published, never in the agreement, never in the chatbot's replies.

⚠️ **§14.7 — tell Ambassadors plainly why the visit check exists. No hidden enforcement.**

---

## 7. HOW THEY LEAVE

**They opt out**, declared, with the 48-hour hold and two years of opt-out payments at 20% of what the current Ambassador earns (§14.16–14.22).

**Or they lapse.** The enforcement chain:

| | |
|---|---|
| §16.1 | Warning **2 weeks before the deadline, daily** until resolved. A chatbot fields replies. |
| ⚠️ §16.2 | **Expiry warnings override the one-note-per-month cap.** This is legal notice. |
| §16.3 | Every warning **logged** with timestamp and delivery status. |
| §16.4 | **6 months past the deadline, the account is released.** |
| §16.5 | **5-day grace** before reassignment begins. |
| §16.6 | Offered within **20 miles**, **admin-adjustable**, ranked by most Hangouts first, **5 Ambassadors max, 5 days each.** |
| §16.7 | Once accepted, **exclusively locked.** |
| ⚠️ §16.8 | **The point of no return is the replacement's first verified visit**, not the offer. |
| §16.13 | A released-then-reoffered Hangout returns to the **front** of the queue. |
| §16.14 | **After five declines, Say Hello phones the business directly.** |
| §16.16 | Dead-letter — everyone declined — is a **recruiting list, not an error state.** |

⚠️ **§16.11 — concentration is intentional.** §16.12 — **declines are data, not discipline.**

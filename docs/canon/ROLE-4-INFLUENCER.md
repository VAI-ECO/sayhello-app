# ROLE 4 — THE INFLUENCER

**The creator whose audience signs up on their code.**

⚠️ Also called Creator on public surfaces. Same role.

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

Somebody with an audience who sends people to Say Hello. Gig and side-hustle creators are the natural fit — the Ambassador opportunity is the story, and it is an unusually clean one, because walking into a store with nothing to hard-sell is a rare pitch.

They do not sign businesses themselves. They send **people who will**.

---

## 2. HOW THEY GET IN

They apply, and they get a **code**.

⚠️ **§2.10 — codes carry credit, never price.** No audience discount, no creator discount, no promo discount. A code attributes; it never reduces what anybody pays.

---

## 3. WHAT THEY CAN DO

**Share a code.**

**Recruit Ambassadors.** ⚠️ This is where the $100 creator-code bonus lives — but it goes to the **Ambassador**, not the Influencer (§2.8).

**Send businesses directly.** A business signing on an Influencer's code earns the Influencer $20/year recurring (§2.7).

**See their dashboard** — earnings, code performance, toolkit.

---

## 4. WHAT THEY CANNOT DO

| |
|---|
| ⚠️ **Offer a discount.** §2.10. Codes carry credit, never price. |
| ⚠️ **Earn a percentage.** §2.12 — **the Influencer rate is $20 flat. NEVER a percentage, anywhere, including comments and variable names.** |
| **Negotiate a different rate.** §2.13 — one rate for everyone. ⚠️ Store the rate on the payout record anyway. |
| **Be paid before the business pays.** §2.14 — nobody in the chain is paid until the Hangout pays Say Hello. |
| ⚠️ **Publish an earnings figure that has not been cleared.** Every figure is MA-05 gated. |

---

## 5. MONEY

⚠️ **$20/year per Hangout, flat, paid by Say Hello on top, for life on both streams** (§2.6 · §2.15).

| Route | What they earn |
|---|---|
| Business signs directly on their code | **$20/year, recurring** (§2.7) |
| An Ambassador signs up on their code | ⚠️ The **$100 bonus goes to the Ambassador**, not the Influencer (§2.8) |

⚠️ **§2.6 says per year. §2.7 says recurring.** Both mean every year the Hangout renews — not year one only. This was found broken in code on 2026-08-16 and fixed.

**§2.14 — nobody is paid until the Hangout pays Say Hello.** Once a year.

---

## 6. WHAT THEY SEE

**A dashboard** — earnings, code performance, an account page.

**A toolkit.** ⚠️ It carries a **do-not-say list**, and that list is MA-05 gated. Every earnings figure a creator might repeat on camera goes through legal first.

⚠️ **EARNED must never look as certain as BUILDING.** Legal, not decorative.

---

## 7. HOW THEY LEAVE

❓ **Unruled.** Nothing in canon covers an Influencer leaving.

Open questions: do recurring $20 payments continue on Hangouts they originated, the way an Ambassador's opt-out payments (20% of what the current Ambassador earns, two years, originated only — §14.16) do? Does the code die, or keep attributing?

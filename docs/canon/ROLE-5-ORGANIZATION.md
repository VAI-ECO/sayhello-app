# ROLE 5 — THE ORGANIZATION

**Churches, clubs, leagues, student groups.**

⚠️ **You said four roles and a core app.** If an Organization is a kind of Hello Hangout rather than its own thing, this file folds into `ROLE-2`. Everything below says it is genuinely separate — but **confirm it**.

---

## 1. WHAT THEY ARE

⚠️ **An organization is not a person.** That single rule shapes everything.

| Has | Does NOT have |
|---|---|
| Hootzas | ❌ Connections |
| A page | ❌ Hellos |
| Followers | ❌ A selfie |
| Check-ins | ❌ A pin on the Hello Map |
| A meeting place | ❌ Points |
| A code | ❌ Icebreakers |
| | ❌ An identity colour |
| | ❌ A drawing entry |

⚠️ **Nothing in the encounter flow touches an organization. Ever.**

**Waverly never nudges on behalf of one.** The people who attend a meeting have their own accounts and their own hellos — **the organization just puts them in the same room.**

That is the whole product logic: an organization is a **noticeboard**, and the density it creates is what makes hellos happen.

---

## 2. HOW THEY GET IN

**Four screens. Under two minutes.** No approval queue, no documents, no waiting.

⚠️ **The person signing up already has a personal account.** They are logged in, and the organization is a **role added to that account** (§26.4), never a separate login.

---

## 3. WHAT THEY CAN DO

**Host Hootzas.** The core of what an organization is for. ⚠️ A Hootza posted to What's Happening consumes a feed slot; one not posted is free (§17.20–17.21).

**Have a page.** People can find them.

**Accumulate followers.**

**Take check-ins.**

**Have a code.**

**Post to What's Happening.** ⚠️ **Same feed ration as a regular Hangout — two free posts per calendar month, $10 after** (§17.10).

---

## 4. WHAT THEY CANNOT DO

| |
|---|
| ⚠️ **Appear on the Hello Map.** They have no address in the encounter sense. |
| ⚠️ **Be part of an encounter.** No connections, no hellos, no nudges. |
| ⚠️ **Earn points or drawing entries.** |
| ⚠️ **Treat followers as a mailing list.** No phone number, email or name ever leaves the app to an organization. |
| ⚠️ **Take money through Say Hello.** **Say Hello never touches money here.** Cover charges at an organization's Hootza are display-only, collected by them, outside the app. |

---

## 5. MONEY

⚠️ **None. In either direction.**

Say Hello does not charge organizations and does not process anything on their behalf. That is deliberate — it removes the entire payment-processing and liability question from a category that includes churches and student groups.

---

## 6. WHAT THEY SEE

Their page, their Hootzas, their follower count, their check-ins.

⚠️ **Not a follower list with contact details.** A count, not a roster.

---

## 7. HOW THEY LEAVE

❓ **Unruled.** The role is additive on a personal account, so removing it should leave the person's account intact — but what happens to their page, their past Hootzas and their followers is not written.
